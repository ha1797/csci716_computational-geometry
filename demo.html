Instructions

Follow these steps to test the system:

  • Use the sample, paste your own segments, or upload a .txt file in the same format as the command-line tool.
  • Click "Run Sweep". The input is validated: no vertical or horizontal segments, no colinear segments, and no three segments through one point.
  • Press "Play" or step through the events. The sweep line moves from right to left.
  • Blue segments are in the sweep-line status, red dots are intersections found so far, and an orange ring marks the current intersection event.
  • Review the output console for results and error logs, or download the intersections file.

Input

First line: number of segments. Then one segment per line as x1 y1 x2 y2.
